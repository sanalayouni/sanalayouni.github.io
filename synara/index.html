Synara

An NLP system that reads every incoming email, works out what it is about and routes it to the right department, with a confidence score on each prediction and a person in the loop for the uncertain ones.

What it is

Support inboxes mix IT problems, invoices, leave requests, angry customers and contracts. Sorting them by hand is slow, and keyword rules break as soon as someone words a request differently. Synara reads the language instead: a text classifier takes the subject and body of each email, works out what the person is asking for, and sends it to IT Support, Finance, HR, Support or Legal.

Because the model can be wrong, it also says how sure it is. Predictions above a confidence threshold are routed automatically, the rest wait in a review queue, and every correction a person makes is logged as new training data. That feedback loop is what makes accuracy climb over time instead of standing still.

How an email is routed

  1. New email
  2. ›
  3. Read the text
  4. ›
  5. Classify the intent
  6. ›
  7. Score the confidence
  8. ›
  9. Route or review

Corrections from the review queue go back into training, so the classifier keeps getting better at the wording this particular inbox receives.

Features

  • OverviewEmails today, routing accuracy, emails waiting for review and average confidence, with the week's volume.
  • Review queueEach email with the predicted department and the model's confidence; approve it or reroute it in one click. A reroute is a labelled example for the next round of training.
  • DepartmentsVolume and routing accuracy for each team.
  • AnalyticsTotal processed, share routed automatically, corrections by department and the accuracy trend.
  • Model settingsConnect the inbox, pick the classification model, set the confidence threshold that decides auto-routing, and turn the feedback loop on.
